TOP HALF: THE FIGHTOne ship, one enemy at a time. The ship holds still, the enemy comes straight down at it, they trade fire, it dies, the next one arrives. Stage after stage, faster and faster.
BOTTOM HALF: THE BUNKERThe upgrade station. It never fights and nothing hits it. Its panel shows the next part, what it changes in numbers, which foes it unlocks and what it costs. You click SEND PART.
HOW A PART REACHES THE SHIPTeleport, while the fight goes on: the launcher charges, a transfer beam fires up to a portal beside the ship, the capsule steps out, clamps on and welds, a scan sweep rebuilds the hull and the new numbers appear.

PLAY IT
Kill enemies: every kill pays SCRAP, POWER and SIGNAL, more for bigger enemies (coins fly to the counters, +n/s shows the income). Click SEND PART in the bunker when the bars are full. Click the enemy to hit it for double damage. A gold supply drone drifts down now and then: click it for extra SCRAP and SIGNAL.
WHAT THE NUMBERS DO
DMG: damage of each bullet. xN SHOTS: bullets per volley, one from every gun on the hull.
RATE: volleys per second, set by the engines.
ARMOR: share of every hit absorbed by shields and plates. HP: what the hull takes. DODGE: chance a hit misses.
SCRAP = hulls and plates, POWER = engines and shields, SIGNAL = guns and masts.
SPEED
Each duel takes about 4 seconds at x1. At x16 the game clears about 4 stages per second and at the top speed, x24, about 9: the enemy changes every tenth of a second. The stars, debris and engine trail stretch with the speed, shots become steady beams, and the duel still reads through the enemy's name and HP bar, hit arcs on every shot, debris that piles up, stacked damage numbers, a kill log and the stage ladder. WAVE and S/S (stages per second) are in the HUD.
